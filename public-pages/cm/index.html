01 / PROGRAM OVERVIEW

The work behind
what we build.

The Stock Development Department of Construction Management is part of FGCU’s U.A. Whitaker College of Engineering.

Why construction management?

Construction brings together planning, technical knowledge, communication, and teamwork. It offers a way to work with both the details of a project and the broader decisions that move it forward.

FGCU’s B.S.C.M. program covers commercial and residential construction, sustainability, and team-based learning. The undergraduate program is accredited by the American Council for Construction Education (ACCE).

Sources, calculation & privacy notes

Prepared from anonymous summaries of program-provided summer internship rosters and senior exit survey information, reviewed October 11, 2026. Internship coverage is Summer 2023 through Summer 2026; exit-survey submission years are 2023–2026. No source workbooks, individual responses, names, contacts, evaluations, or employer-specific wages are published.

Internship entries are counted once per student per summer term. Employer names are consolidated across spelling, punctuation, abbreviations, and known company aliases, including Owen-Ames-Kimball / O-A-K and Manhattan / Manhattan Construction. Uncertain matches are kept separate. Named employer groups require at least five distinct interns across the full reporting period; smaller groups and detailed term breakdowns are omitted. The mean and median use 212 positive numeric hourly rates. Forty-eight missing, zero, nonnumeric, or ambiguous/non-hourly amounts are excluded; no annual salary is converted to an hourly rate. Employer counts and wage calculations have different sample sizes because some records lack an employer name.

Exit-survey offer percentages use all 204 yes/no responses to the job-offer question. Only employer categories with at least five responses are displayed; other and missing categories are omitted. These descriptive samples are not guarantees of wages, placements, or career outcomes.
